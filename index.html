Frequently asked questions

Is Fulcrum free?

Fulcrum is free to download, and new users receive a welcome bonus of Challenge Coins to start their first challenge. Additional Challenge Coins are available as optional in-app purchases.

How does the token system work?

To start a challenge you pre-consume a number of tokens (Challenge Coins). If you stay within your rules until the challenge ends, you receive exactly the same amount back. If you give in and open the locked apps, the consumed tokens are permanently lost.

How does Fulcrum verify that I completed a challenge?

Fulcrum monitors the apps and categories you selected locally on your device using Apple’s Screen Time technology, so the result of every challenge is determined on-device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Challenge records are stored on your device or in your private iCloud. Cloud servers only keep anonymized transaction logs for accounting, and if you delete your account your identity data is permanently detached.

Which devices and languages are supported?

Fulcrum requires iOS 18.2 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. It’s available in English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, French, Spanish, German, Brazilian Portuguese and Arabic.
